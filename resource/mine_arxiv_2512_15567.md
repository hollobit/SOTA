# arxiv 2512.15567 — Evaluating LLMs in Scientific Discovery

## Paper summary
- **Authors / affiliation**: Zhangde Song, Jieyu Lu, Yuanqi Du, Botao Yu, Thomas M. Pruyn, Yue Huang, Kehan Guo, et al. — Deep Principle (Hangzhou), Cornell, Ohio State, Toronto, Notre Dame, QuEra Computing, Stanford Med, Harvard, Tufts, Georgia Tech, EPFL, UCLA, Virginia Tech, Tsinghua, Princeton, Harvard SEAS, Vector Institute, UT Austin, McGill, USTC, CUHK Shenzhen, MIT, Zhejiang U, HKUST, Cambridge, WashU St. Louis, UC Berkeley/UCLA. Correspondence: haojunjia@deepprinciple.com, sun.397@osu.edu, mohamad.moosavi@utoronto.ca, duanchenru@gmail.com.
- **Date**: arXiv v2, 8 May 2026 (cs.AI 2512.15567).
- **Bench introduced**: 
  - **SDE (Scientific Discovery Evaluation)** — scenario-grounded benchmark across 4 domains, 8 projects, 43 scenarios, **1,125 questions** total. Question-level eval via lm-evaluation-harness fork; project-level eval via sde-harness. Datasets at HF deep-principle/datasets; code at github.com/deepprinciple/lm-evaluation-harness and github.com/HowieHwong/sde-harness.
  - **SDE-hard** — 86-question subset (2 hardest per scenario where top LLMs make most mistakes).
  - Domain breakdown: Chemistry 276 q / 12 scenarios; Materials 486 q / 13 scenarios; Biology 200 q / 10 scenarios; Physics 163 q / 8 scenarios.
  - Metric: exact-match accuracy (case/punctuation insensitive); tolerance windows for numeric (e.g., redox 0.25 band); Tanimoto similarity on RDKit-canonicalized SMILES for structure tasks; math_verify for symbolic physics; partial credit for CRISPR delivery and lattice prediction.

## New benchmark IDs to register
- **sde** — Scientific Discovery Evaluation, 1,125 question-level items across biology/chemistry/materials/physics, 43 scenarios, 8 projects — category: science / discovery — metric: exact-match accuracy (mean over scenarios)
- **sde-biology** — 200 questions, 10 scenarios (Lipinski, enzymatic-reaction, GWAS-causal-gene, gene-editing-protocol, CRISPR-delivery, descriptor-prediction, fragment-completion, matched-pair, property-matching, protein-localization) — category: biology — metric: accuracy
- **sde-chemistry** — 276 questions, 12 scenarios (forward-reaction, retrosynthesis, molecular-property, NMR-to-structure, IR-to-structure, MS-peak, experimental-techniques, quantum-chem-software, reaction-mechanism, TMC-properties, redox-potential, MW-to-formula) — category: chemistry — metric: accuracy
- **sde-materials** — 486 questions, 13 scenarios (corrosion, safety, PXRD-system, PXRD-lattice, MOF-water-stability, MOF-synthesis, battery-electrolyte, biomaterials, composite, general-materials, LAMMPS-VASP, polymer-glass, PXRD-denoising) — category: materials — metric: accuracy
- **sde-physics** — 163 questions, 8 scenarios (astrophysics-cosmology, quantum-info, condensed-matter, high-energy, statistics, computation, core-knowledge, cosmology-equations) — category: physics — metric: accuracy
- **sde-hard** — 86 questions (2 hardest per scenario) — category: science / discovery — metric: accuracy (strict)
- **sde-harness-protein-design** — project-level — metric: norm. Top-1 fitness over GB1/TrpB/Syn-3bfo/AAV/GFP
- **sde-harness-gene-editing** — IFNG perturbation 5-round 128-gene panel — metric: Mean Hit Rate %
- **sde-harness-retrosynthesis** — Pistachio Hard 100 targets — metric: solve rate %
- **sde-harness-molecule-optimization** — jnk3 / gsk3β / sitagliptin_mpo — metric: AUC-top-10 / Avg-top-10
- **sde-harness-tmc-optimization** — 1.37M Pd(II) TMC pool, 20-iter evolutionary search — metric: norm. polarisability + Pareto frontier (proprietary aggregate)
- **sde-harness-crystal-design** — MatLLMSearch over MatBench-bandgap — metric: Metastability % / S.U.N. Rate %
- **sde-harness-symbolic-regression** — physics nonlinear-dynamics dataset (PySR benchmark) — metric: Acc@0.1 ID/OOD + NMSE
- **sde-harness-ising** — N=12 all-to-all Ising — metric: min energy gap (lower better; GT=-19.98)
