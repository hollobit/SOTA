# Agent-World (2604.18292) — Deep Mining Findings

## Paper claim summary
- **Is "agent_world" a registrable benchmark?** NO. "Agent-World" is presented as a *training arena / environment-synthesis framework* and as a *model series* (Agent-World-8B, Agent-World-14B trained on Qwen3-8B/14B). The paper does NOT report any "Agent-World" composite score that other models could be measured against. The 23 benchmarks listed are evaluation suites the Agent-World model is *tested on*, not a single new benchmark.
- **Frameworks vs models — does paper attribute scores to specific model_ids?** YES, partially. Table 1 (page 14) contains a strict (model × sub-benchmark × value) score matrix for 13 models on MCP-Mark (5 sub-tasks + Avg), BFCL V4 (7 sub-categories + Avg), and τ²-Bench (3 sub-domains + Avg). Table 2 (page 16) contains a small (model × benchmark × value) matrix for self-evolution rounds. Figures 6, 7, 8 contain additional per-benchmark scores but are graphical and not parseable from text extraction.
